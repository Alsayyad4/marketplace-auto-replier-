**Description**
```
SubSell Auto-Reply helps a single seller keep up with their own Facebook
Marketplace / Messenger buyer messages. When you turn it ON, it reads the latest
message in each of your conversations and drafts a reply with the Anthropic Claude
API, in your voice and your language (auto-detects French or English — casual
Quebec French supported).

You stay in control:
• Your Anthropic API key, your prompts, your listings and prices.
• Business hours, hourly/daily caps, and a per-conversation reply limit.
• Human-like typing speed and cadence so replies don't look robotic.
• Escalation: anything risky (scams, shipping/payment, "contact me off-platform")
  is handed back to you instead of auto-answered.
• Optional demo-video sending, follow-up nudges, and a "closer" mode that steers
  buyers toward an in-person visit.

How it works
• Configure once in the dashboard; the same settings can apply to all your
  computers.
• The extension does the replying inside your own Messenger — nothing is posted on
  your behalf anywhere else.

Privacy
• No data-collecting server. The extension talks only to Anthropic (to generate
  replies), to Facebook/Messenger (your own chats), and — only if you enable it — to
  your own cloud project. Your API key and settings stay on your machine (or in a
  cloud project you control). Nothing is sold or used for ads. See the privacy
  policy for details.

Note: you need your own Anthropic API key (from console.anthropic.com).
```

**Single purpose**
```
Auto-replies to the operator's own Facebook Marketplace buyer messages using the
Anthropic Claude API.
```
